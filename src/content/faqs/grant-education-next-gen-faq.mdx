faqSections:
  - heading: 'Before you apply'
    items:
      - question: 'Who is eligible to apply?'
        answer: "The program is open to accredited or officially recognized higher education institutions that want to create or expand educational opportunities for tertiary-level students in Open Payments, interoperability, digital financial inclusion, fintech, and related areas.<br />\_\n\nApplications should be submitted on behalf of the institution and led by an appropriate faculty member, department, school, center, research unit, or other recognized institutional unit.<br />"
      - question: 'Can student clubs apply directly?'
        answer: 'The NextGen Higher Education Grant is designed as a&#x6E;**&#x20;institutional grant**. Student clubs may participate in or help implement project activities, but an eligible higher education institution or recognized institutional unit must submit and manage the application.'
      - question: 'Can institutions outside the United States apply?'
        answer: "Yes. Eligible higher education institutions may apply regardless of country, subject to applicable Interledger Foundation eligibility and due diligence requirements.<br />\_\n\nInstitutions must be able to demonstrate appropriate accreditation or official recognition in the country where they operate."
      - question: 'Does our institution need previous experience with Interledger?'
        answer: "No. Previous experience with Interledger Protocol, Open Payments, or related technologies is welcome but is not required.<br />\_\n\nInstitutions that are new to the ecosystem should demonstrate a credible plan for building the knowledge and capacity needed to deliver the proposed program successfully."
      - question: 'How much funding is available?'
        answer: "Selected institutions may request up to **USD $50,000.**<br />\_\n\nThe funding request should be proportionate to the scope of the proposed project, anticipated number of students, planned activities, and expected outcomes.<br />"
      - question: 'Where can I learn more before applying?'
        answer: "Applicants are encouraged to review the **NextGen Higher Education Program Resource Kit** before preparing their Letter of Intent.\n\nThe Resource Kit provides additional guidance to help institutions understand the NextGen program and prepare a strong application. It includes information on program objectives and eligibility, preparing the Letter of Intent, [Open Payments](https://interledger.org/open-payments) and Interledger resources, application expectations, and the full proposal process.\n\n<br />"
