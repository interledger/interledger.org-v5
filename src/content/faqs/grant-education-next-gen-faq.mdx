faqSections:
  - heading: 'Before you apply'
    items:
      - question: 'Who is eligible to apply?'
        answer: "The program is open to accredited or officially recognized higher education institutions that want to create or expand educational opportunities for tertiary-level students in Open Payments, interoperability, digital financial inclusion, fintech, and related areas.<br />\_\n\nApplications should be submitted on behalf of the institution and led by an appropriate faculty member, department, school, center, research unit, or other recognized institutional unit.<br />"
      - question: 'Can student clubs apply directly?'
        answer: 'The NextGen Higher Education Grant is designed as a&#x6E;**&#x20;institutional grant**. Student clubs may participate in or help implement project activities, but an eligible higher education institution or recognized institutional unit must submit and manage the application.'
      - question: 'Can institutions outside the United States apply?'
        answer: "Yes. Eligible higher education institutions may apply regardless of country, subject to applicable Interledger Foundation eligibility and due diligence requirements.<br />\_\n\nInstitutions must be able to demonstrate appropriate accreditation or official recognition in the country where they operate."
      - question: 'Does our institution need previous experience with Interledger?'
        answer: "No. Previous experience with Interledger Protocol, Open Payments, or related technologies is welcome but is not required.<br />\_\n\nInstitutions that are new to the ecosystem should demonstrate a credible plan for building the knowledge and capacity needed to deliver the proposed program successfully."
      - question: 'How much funding is available?'
        answer: "Selected institutions may request up to **USD $50,000.**<br />\_\n\nThe funding request should be proportionate to the scope of the proposed project, anticipated number of students, planned activities, and expected outcomes.<br />"
      - question: 'Where can I learn more before applying?'
        answer: "Applicants are encouraged to review the **NextGen Higher Education Program Resource Kit** before preparing their Letter of Intent.\n\nThe Resource Kit provides additional guidance to help institutions understand the NextGen program and prepare a strong application. It includes information on program objectives and eligibility, preparing the Letter of Intent, [Open Payments](/tech/open-payments) and Interledger resources, application expectations, and the full proposal process.\n\n<br />"
  - heading: 'About NextGen funded projects'
    items:
      - question: 'How long can a project run?'
        answer: "Projects may be implemented for a maximum of 12 months.<br />\_\n\nApplicants should propose a timeline that is realistic for the project activities and intended outcomes."
      - question: 'What types of projects can be funded?'
        answer: "Projects should create meaningful educational opportunities for students to learn about, explore, research, or apply concepts related to [Open Payments](/tech/open-payments) and digital financial inclusion.\n\nProjects may include:\n\n* Development of new courses;\n* Revision of existing curricula;\n* Workshops and seminars;\n* Technical labs;\n* Hackathons or innovation challenges;\n* Student research;\n* Capstone projects;\n* Prototypes or demonstrations;\n* Guest lectures;\n* Interdisciplinary learning programs; and\n* Other co-curricular or experiential learning activities.\n\nProjects should form a coherent educational program rather than a series of unrelated events."
      - question: 'Does the project need to include a formal university course?'
        answer: "No. A project may include formal curriculum development, co-curricular activities, or a combination of both.\n\nHowever, all proposed activities should have clear educational objectives and provide meaningful learning opportunities for tertiary-level students."
      - question: 'How should Open Payments and Interledger be incorporated into the project?'
        answer: "Students should receive meaningful exposure to [Open Payments](/tech/open-payments), interoperability, digital financial inclusion, and relevant Interledger concepts or technologies.\n\nDepending on the project, students may:\n\n* Learn foundational concepts;\n* Explore payment use cases;\n* Participate in technical demonstrations or labs;\n* Work with Open Payments APIs;\n* Build or test prototypes;\n* Conduct research;\n* Participate in hackathons or innovation challenges; or\n* Apply these concepts through coursework, assignments, or student projects.\n\nThe level of technical engagement should be appropriate for the students and the proposed program.\n\n<br />"
      - question: 'What support can the Interledger Foundation provide?'
        answer: 'In addition to grant funding, selected institutions may identify support areas that could strengthen successful project implementation, such as technical orientation, learning resources, guidance on Open Payments and Interledger, guest speakers, ecosystem connections, technical mentorship, or faculty support. Projects should, however, be designed with sufficient institutional capacity to operate effectively without relying extensively on ongoing Interledger Foundation staff support.'
      - question: 'What kinds of student outcomes are expected?'
        answer: 'Project outcomes will vary based on the nature and scope of each initiative, but institutions should clearly define the learning objectives and expected student outputs. These may include increased understanding of Open Payments and digital financial inclusion, development of technical or research skills, student prototypes, research papers, case studies, presentations, capstone projects, technical demonstrations, open-source contributions, or other documented evidence of student learning and engagement.'
