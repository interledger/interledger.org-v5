## **Two Ways to Take Part**

When you [submit your interest](https://airtable.com/appYUC3JTQeMVH9uS/pag79anbx7g4DTCi6/form), you can indicate whether you would like to serve in either role, or both:

- **Selection Committee Member**: help shape which proposals move forward, assessing them against the published criteria for the cycle. Reviews will be assigned randomly based on your expertise and availability.
- **Peer Reviewer**: lend your expertise to peer review selected final abstracts at the midterm and end of the grant that fall specifically within your area of expertise.

If you are invited to join the Selection Committee or contacted to be a Peer Reviewer, you will receive an honorarium in appreciation of your expertise and service. 

We intend to form a pool of qualified individuals from this EOI process and draw from it based on the applications received and/or the topics of papers to review, contacting panel members with aligned interests and expertise
