## **Connected Finance Starts Here**

The **Interledger–NextGen Higher Education Program** supports higher education institutions in advancing curriculum development, research, and hands-on learning in Open Payments, interoperability, and inclusive digital finance across disciplines such as finance, economics, business, law, engineering, and technology.

Through the program, institutions can create or strengthen academic and co-curricular experiences that introduce students to emerging financial technologies, the Interledger ecosystem, and pathways into careers and research shaping the future of digital finance.

Selected institutions may receive grants of up to **USD $50,000** to implement and complete projects within **12 months**.
