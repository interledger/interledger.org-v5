## **How to apply**

For **Phase 1**, we ask all interested universities to submit:

1. A short application form via Submittable
2. Letter of Intent (LOI) / Project Introduction Letter
3. Proof of institutional accreditation or recognition

Applicants with a strong Letter of Intent will be invited to Phase 2, where a full proposal will be needed. A detailed budget, implementation plan, and full project proposal are **not required&#x20;**&#x64;uring Phase 1. 

---

**Please note:&#x20;**&#x52;eview the **NextGen Program Resource Kit (LINK)&#x20;**&#x66;or program guidance and resources to help you develop your Letter of Intent. 

To apply for the NextGen Higher Education Program, please create an account on [Submittable](https://submit.interledger.org/login) and complete the application and upload your Letter of Intent. Applications for Phase 1 will be open until **December 14, 2026**.

## **Impact Stories&#xA0;**

Open financial systems require people who understand how payments move across technologies, markets, and regulatory environments. The Interledger Foundation supports institutions developing the research, teaching, and practical learning needed to prepare students to understand, research and contribute to modern financial systems..
