## **What Fellows Get**

- $72,000 fellowship stipend across the program year
- $20,000 project funding to support research, development, or advocacy work
- Access to the Interledger Foundation's global network of researchers, developers, policymakers, and institutions
- Peer learning and collaboration with the current fellowship cohort
- Opportunities to present work at the Interledger Summit and other Foundation events
- Institutional support and mentorship throughout the program

</Paragraph>

<Paragraph>

## **What Are We Looking For?**

Strong proposals address real barriers that prevent people from participating fully in digital financial systems. These may include the cost of transactions, documentation requirements, absent or inadequate infrastructure, regulatory frameworks that exclude rather than protect, or a lack of accessible tools and knowledge. We fund work across five areas:

</Paragraph>
