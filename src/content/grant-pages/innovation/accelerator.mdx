<Paragraph>

## What Grantees Get

- $50,000 in funding
- Four months to build a proof of concept with structured support
- Technical workshops and mentorship throughout the program
- Cohort learning and access to the Interledger ecosystem
- Opportunity to showcase proof of concept at the virtual Demo Day in November
- Selected teams receive additional funding and the opportunity to present at the Interledger Summit

</Paragraph>

<Paragraph>

## What We’re Looking for

Strong proposals start with a real problem that a specific community is experiencing today and a team that understands that community directly. Beyond that, we look for technical capacity to build toward a proof of concept in four months, intentional use of Interledger or Open Payments, and at least early evidence that the solution is grounded in real user needs. We do not expect teams to have everything figured out, and indeed this program is designed to help you develop and refine. What we need to see is a genuine problem, a strong team, and a credible direction.

</Paragraph>
