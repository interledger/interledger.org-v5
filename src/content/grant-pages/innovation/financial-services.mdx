## **What We Are Looking to Build**

We want to support institutions that can demonstrate how our [open, interoperable payment infrastructure](/tech/overview/) can work in practice: connecting systems, establishing network relationships, testing transactions, serving customers, and generating lessons that can support future implementations. By bringing more financial service providers into interoperable networks, the projects we fund can help advance the Interledger Foundation’s mission to foster an inclusive global financial system.

Our interoperable payments infrastructure includes Rhyza - a free, open source software that connects a financial service provider’s existing infrastructure to Interledger, so it can make payments across systems and currencies and offer Open Payments-enabled accounts and services. Open Payments is an API standard that enables secure, consent-based payment initiation and management. Together, these technologies support accessible, interoperable payment services. 

## **How to Apply**

Applications for Phase 1 are open until **Friday, December 11, 2026 at 11:59 pm ET.**

For Phase 1, we ask all interested organizations to [submit a short application form](https://submit.interledger.org/submit/369477/2027-digital-financial-services-grant-expression-of-interest/eligibility), providing key details about your proposal.  Applicants with a strong Phase 1 submission will be invited to Phase 2, where a more detailed proposal shall be requested. Phase 2 is anticipated to run from February 1 to 26, 2027. 

Please review the [2027 Digital Financial Services Grant, EOI Resource Kit  ](https://drive.google.com/drive/u/0/folders/1drmAFOrsQxVKOyKxGrKN6YXca2w6ppFt)and [our full FAQ](/grant/innovation/financial-services/faq/) for program guidance and resources to help you develop your proposals for Phase 1.

### People's Clearinghouse

People's Clearinghouse (PCH) is a digital payments platform that seeks to connect Mexico's community banks and savings cooperatives with the national financial system and international remittance networks. Led by AMUCSS and built on open-source Interledger technology, the project is working to route remittances straight into community bank accounts instead of cash, so money stays local as savings and credit.

With support from the Interledger Foundation, PCH has grown into a production-ready platform, demonstrated live at the 2025 Interledger Summit in Mexico City. It expects Central Bank authorization and launch by the end of 2026, reaching several hundred thousand people across its partner network, many gaining access to digital payments for the first time.
