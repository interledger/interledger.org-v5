## Two Tracks of Funding

Two grant tracks are available, depending on the type of integration:

</Paragraph>

<CardGrid ariaLabel="Two grant tracks for Digital Financial Services" variant="Info" columns="Two">
<InfoCard heading="Interledger | Enabled (up to $250,000 USD)">

For digital wallets, mobile money providers, banks, savings co-ops, and neo-banks integrating via [Rafiki](https://rafiki.dev/), the open-source reference implementation of the Interledger Protocol and Open Payments standard. This track supports providers establishing a new node on the Interledger network.

</InfoCard>
<InfoCard heading="Interledger | Unlocked (up to $150,000 USD)">

For web-based platforms, including e-commerce sites and donation platforms, integrating the [Open Payments API](https://openpayments.dev/) to expand payment options for their users.

</InfoCard>
</CardGrid>

## What Grantees Get

- Grant funding for technical development and integration
- Access to Interledger Foundation staff support throughout the integration process
- Connection to a global community of fellow grantees and open payments practitioners
- Technical resources and documentation via the [Interledger developer portal](/tech/dev-portal)
- Opportunities to present work at the [Interledger Summit](/summit)

### People's Clearinghouse

People's Clearinghouse (PCH) is a digital payments platform that seeks to connect Mexico's community banks and savings cooperatives with the national financial system and international remittance networks. Led by AMUCSS and built on open-source Interledger technology, the project is working to route remittances straight into community bank accounts instead of cash, so money stays local as savings and credit.

With support from the Interledger Foundation, PCH has grown into a production-ready platform, demonstrated live at the 2025 Interledger Summit in Mexico City. It expects Central Bank authorization and launch by the end of 2026, reaching several hundred thousand people across its partner network, many gaining access to digital payments for the first time.
