<Paragraph>

Sessions at the Summit referenced a number of data points on the cost of cross-border payments today.[^1] The sources for those figures are collected below.[^2] [^3]

[^1]: Average cost to send 1 USDT (Tether, the largest stablecoin pegged to the US dollar) on August 5, 2025 per [GasFeesNow](https://gasfeesnow.com).

[^2]: Average price for a $200 bank initiated cross-border transfer, according to the [World Bank](https://remittanceprices.worldbank.org) (Q3, 2024).

[^3]: Average price to send $200 from the United States to Mexico using Wise for a personal account to a personal account transaction, according to the Inter-American Dialogue (2024).
