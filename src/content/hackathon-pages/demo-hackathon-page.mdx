<Paragraph>

This hackathon demo page covers the dynamic-zone set available to hackathon
content. The sample copy is written like event content so spacing, hierarchy,
and component previews feel close to the real site.

Blocks covered on this page:

- Paragraph and split layout (image / video)
- Tables (fee comparison below)
- Number tiles
- Image block with desktop and tablet sources
- **Card grid** — all variants: Title, Resource, Info, Navigation
- Profile grid
- Logo carousel

Use it to validate challenge copy, resource links, mentor grids, and partner
logos. Teams often compare prototype transfer costs to traditional rails.[^1]
Typical remittance quotes sit well above Interledger settlement.[^2] Fintech
corridors have narrowed that gap but still leave a premium.[^3]

[^1]: Average cost to send 1 USDT (Tether, the largest stablecoin pegged to the US dollar) on August 5, 2025 per GasFeesNow. [https://gasfeesnow.com](https://gasfeesnow.com/)

[^2]: Average price for a $200 bank initiated cross-border transfer, according to the World Bank (Q3, 2024). [https://remittanceprices.worldbank.org/sites/default/files/rpw_main_report_and_annex_q324.pdf](https://remittanceprices.worldbank.org/sites/default/files/rpw_main_report_and_annex_q324.pdf)

[^3]: Average price to send $200 from the United States to Mexico using Wise for a personal account to a personal account transaction, according to the Inter-American Dialogue (2024). [https://thedialogue.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Transaction-Costs-and-Money-Transfer-Operators-A-Review-of-Costs-by-MTO-Receiving-Countries.pdf](https://thedialogue.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Transaction-Costs-and-Money-Transfer-Operators-A-Review-of-Costs-by-MTO-Receiving-Countries.pdf)

| Method                         | Typical fee (send $200) | Typical settlement time |
| ------------------------------ | ----------------------- | ----------------------- |
| Interledger                    | ~$0.001                 | Seconds                 |
| Traditional bank wire          | $30+                    | 1–3 days                |
| Fintech remittance (e.g. Wise) | ~$8–12                  | Minutes–hours           |

</Paragraph>

## Event card

Hackathon pages can use a full-width **Event card** (When, Where, optional
Apply). One card at a time; without Apply, When and Where expand across the
row.

</Paragraph>

<Faq heading="FAQs – All Hackathons">
  <FaqItem question="Who can join?">
    Anyone over 18 years old who wants to learn more about Open Payments and
    loves to build and tinker is welcome to register.
  </FaqItem>
  <FaqItem question="When does the application window close?">
    The application window closes two weeks before the hackathon.
  </FaqItem>
  <FaqItem question="How can I apply?">
    Register on the hackathon site. Solo participants can form a team at
    kickoff; teams of up to four are welcome.
  </FaqItem>
  <FaqItem question="Do I need prior experience with Open Payments?">
    No. Mentors and a tech orientation cover the basics. Bring curiosity and a
    laptop.
  </FaqItem>
</Faq>
