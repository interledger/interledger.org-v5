<Paragraph>

This hackathon demo page covers the dynamic-zone set available to hackathon
content. The sample copy is written like event content so spacing, hierarchy,
and component previews feel close to the real site.

Blocks covered on this page:

- Paragraph and split layout (image / video)
- **Card grid** — all variants: Title, Resource, Info, Navigation
- Profile grid
- Logo carousel

Use it to validate challenge copy, resource links, mentor grids, and partner
logos.

## Event card

Hackathon pages can use a full-width **Event card** (When, Where, optional
Apply). One card at a time; without Apply, When and Where expand across the
row.

</Paragraph>

<EventCard>
  <EventWhen title="When?" date="November 6–7, 2026" time="48 hours">
    Build starts after the kickoff briefing.
  </EventWhen>
  <EventWhere title="Where?" location="Hackathon workspace — main hall">

  </EventWhere>
  <EventApply

<Faq>
  <FaqItem question="Do I need a team to enter?">
    No. You can register solo and form a team during the opening session, or
    bring a team of up to four people.
  </FaqItem>
  <FaqItem question="What can I build?">
    Anything that uses Open Payments or Web Monetization. Judges score on
    originality, technical execution, and real-world impact.
  </FaqItem>
