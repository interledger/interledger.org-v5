## Judging Criteria

| Criteria             | What we're looking for                                                                                 |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Problem Definition   | Is the challenge clear and meaningful?                                                                 |
| Use of Open Payments | Does the project demonstrate a working payment flow?                                                   |
| User Experience      | Does the solution create value for users?                                                              |
| Technical Quality    | Does the prototype work reliably?                                                                      |
| Presentation         | Can the team clearly explain what they've built and why it matters?                                    |
| Inclusion            | How does this solution make payments easier, more accessible, or more inclusive for underserved users? |
| Innovation           | How creative and original is this solution's approach to payments?                                     |
| Impact               | What difference could this solution make for users or communities, and how clearly is that explained?  |

### How Judging Works

#### **Round 1**

Every team presents to a judging panel. This takes place in the hack rooms on Saturday at 12:00, after all participants have submitted their final presentations and code. There will be 4 rooms, each with a minimum of 2 judges. Teams present to the judges in their assigned room. Once all teams in a room have presented, the judges deliberate and select the teams to progress to Round 2, based on each team's cumulative score against the judging criteria.

 

#### **Round 2**

The 12 teams progressing to Round 2 are announced in the auditorium and immediately present their solutions to a new judging panel, which includes representatives from each award category. All participants also vote for their favorite solution for the This Week in Fintech Community Award. Once all Round 2 presentations are complete, the judging panel convenes to select the overall award winners. All participants are then invited back into the auditorium for the winners' announcement.
