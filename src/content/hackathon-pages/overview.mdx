<Paragraph>

During an Interledger Open Payments Hackathon, you'll take on a real financial challenge, build a working prototype, and pitch it to a panel of industry leaders, investors and ecosystem partners. You build on **Open Payments**: an open API standard that lets applications send, receive and manage payments across networks through one interoperable interface.

**Registration to our Ecosystem hackathons is open to students, professionals, and hobbyists alike and no prior Interledger experience required. We also support Student Hackathons through our NextGen Higher Education Grantees and partners.**

</Paragraph>

## New to Open Payments? Start here.

### Complete the free Open Payments course on Platzi

Take our beginner Open Payments course and start the hackathon already
knowing the technology.

#### How to Access the Course

1. Go to [platzi.com](https://platzi.com) and create a free account or log
   in.
2. Access the course:
   [Open Payments with Interledger](https://platzi.com/cursos/interledger-openpayments/).
3. Complete the lessons and pass the final exam. (If you fail the exam, you
   must wait 6 hours before retrying.)
4. Afterwards, you can join our community on [Slack](https://join.slack.com/t/interledger/shared_invite/zt-44g089zrn-XdSIiHF~cs8Oo_MBmSfECA) and introduce
   yourself in the #interledger-platzi channel and share your certificate
   (optional).

### Additional Support

We have a wealth of Open Payments resources, from documentation, to guides,
to video series.
