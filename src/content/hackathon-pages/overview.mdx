<Paragraph>

During a 24-hour Interledger Open Payments Hackathon, you'll take on a real
financial challenge, build a working prototype, and pitch it to a panel of
industry leaders, investors and ecosystem partners. You build on Open
Payments: an open API standard that lets applications send, receive and
manage payments across networks through one interoperable interface.
