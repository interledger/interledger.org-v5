<Paragraph>

- You must be 18 or older to participate.
- Teams of 4 to 5 participants can enter.
- This hackathon will take place in English and Spanish.
- Bring your laptop along. We will not provide equipment.
- You need to attend both days/the full 24 hours and demo your solution.
- Our hackathons are all in person: no remote participation is possible.
- There are limited spaces available, so look out for your confirmation of a
  secured spot.
- If you are offered a place, you must accept it within 3 working days, or
  it may be offered to someone else.
- All code needs to be developed within the 2 days of the hackathon.
- We do not pay for transportation and accommodation.

</Paragraph>
