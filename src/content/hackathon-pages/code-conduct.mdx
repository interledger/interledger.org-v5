## Purpose

The Interledger Foundation is dedicated to providing a harassment-free event<br />experience for everyone, regardless of gender, gender identity and<br />expression, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, body size,<br />race, age, religion or lack thereof. We do not tolerate harassment of event<br />participants in any form. Sexual language and imagery are not appropriate<br />for any event venue, including talks. Event participants violating these<br />rules may be sanctioned or expelled from the event at the discretion of the<br />ILF staff.

## Anti-harassment

Harassment includes, but is not limited to:

- Verbal comments that reinforce social structures of domination related to<br /><br /> 

  gender, gender identity and expression, sexual orientation, disability,<br /><br /> 

  physical appearance, body size, race, age, religion

- Sexual images in public spaces

- Deliberate intimidation, stalking, or following

- Harassing photography or recording

- Sustained disruption of talks or other events

- Inappropriate physical contact

- Invasion of personal space

- Unwelcome sexual attention

- Advocating for, or encouraging, any of the above behavior

## Enforcement

Participants asked to stop any harassing behavior are expected to comply<br />immediately.

If a participant engages in harassing behavior, event organizers retain the<br />right to take any actions to keep the event a welcoming environment for all<br />participants. This includes warning the offender or expulsion from the<br />event.

Event organizers may take action to address anything designed to, or with<br />the clear impact of, disrupting the event or making the environment hostile<br />for any participants. We expect participants to follow these rules at all<br />event venues and event-related social activities. We think people should<br />follow these rules outside event activities too!

## Reporting

If someone makes you or anyone else feel unsafe or unwelcome, please report<br />it as soon as possible. Harassment and other Code of Conduct (CoC)<br />violations reduce the value of our event for everyone. We want you to be<br />happy at our event. You can make a report either personally or anonymously.

### Anonymous report

You can [make an anonymous report here](https://forms.gle/j4csyK24srkyra7i6).<br />We, the organizers, can't follow up an anonymous report with you directly,<br />but we will fully investigate it and take whatever action is necessary to<br />prevent a recurrence.

### Personal report

In case of aggression or violence please call security immediately!

If you would like to report an incident, you can do so via email to<br />[coc@interledger.org](mailto:coc@interledger.org) (ILF Team). Emails sent to this address are read by the<br />ILF Code of Conduct (CoC) Team.

**Organizers/Curators**

You can make a personal report by:

- Contacting an ILF TEAM member
- Emailing us: [coc@interledger.org](mailto:coc@interledger.org)

When taking a personal report, our staff will ensure you are safe and<br />cannot be overheard. They may involve other event staff to ensure your<br />report is managed properly. Once safe, we'll ask you to tell us about what<br />happened. This can be upsetting, but we'll handle it as respectfully as<br />possible, and you can bring someone to support you. You won't be asked to<br />confront anyone and we won't tell anyone who you are.

Our team will be happy to help you contact hotel/venue security, local law<br />enforcement, local support services, provide escorts, or otherwise assist<br />you to feel safe for the duration of the event. We value your partnership<br />in organizing/curating the event.

## Inclusive language

In our commitment to a harassment-free and inclusive environment we<br />strongly believe it's important to pay attention to harmful language<br />patterns and an [intersectional](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intersectionality)<br />view on discriminations that rarely happen only one-sided. We understand<br />that our list of isms below is by far not complete and believe it is a<br />basic start to raise awareness at our events.

### Ableism

Words like "crazy", "dumb", "insane" or "lame" are examples of ableist<br />language, devaluing people who have physical or mental disabilities. Its<br />appearance often stems not from any intentional desire to offend, but from<br />our innate sense of what it means to be normal. These words can be avoided<br />by using more fitting, clearer descriptions of what we want to communicate.

To find out more about ableism and replacement terms please read<br />[this guide](http://www.autistichoya.com/p/ableist-words-and-terms-to-avoid.html).

### Sexism

Using gendered terms like "dude" or "guys" to address a mixed-gendered<br />group of people contributes to furthering exclusion of underrepresented<br />individuals. We strongly advise avoiding gendered pronouns as well as<br />gendered terms. If unsure about people's pronouns, we highly recommend to<br />ask people directly for their pronouns first and to check if there are<br />already any hints provided by themselves (e.g. on badges) instead of<br />assuming their pronouns.

For more information please familiarize yourself with the<br />[Geek Feminism wiki guide](http://geekfeminism.wikia.com/wiki/Nonsexist_language)<br />and [Wikipedia Third Person Pronoun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Third-person_pronoun).

### Racism

Racism is deeply rooted in our society and globally, exists among all<br />social classes and is connected to<br />[colonialism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colonialism) which has a long<br />history of violence, oppression, and domination of one group or individual<br />over another group or individual of a different race, ethnicity, culture,<br />and their territories. Addressing individuals or a group of people in a<br />diminutive, derogative, or questioning way based on their (assumed) race<br />and ethnic background is therefore racist, disrespectful, and harmful. We<br />do not tolerate any racist behavior, slurs, statements, or jokes and will<br />take action if any reports on this matter reach us.

For more information please read<br />[Wikipedia Racism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Racism).
