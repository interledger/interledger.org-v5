## How to Prepare

The following guides and resources give you everything you need for the day of the Hackathon.

- **Participant Guide** - all you need to know about participating in the hackathon in a single pdf guide. 
- **Submission Guidelines** - download our detailed instructions on how to submit your code and project for review the days of the hackathon. 
- **Ideation Guide - xxxxxxxxxxxx**
- [**AI Usage Guide**](/hackathon/ai-policy) - read our guidelines on what is allowed when using AI to support your hack. 
- [**Get acquainted with the tech**](/hackathon/open-payments/) - learn about Open Payments, get your test wallet set up, and explore the tools available to you the day of the hackathon.
- **Presentation Deck** - download the presentation template for your final pitch 

**Top Tip:** Start to think about problems with payments - how could you make them easier, simpler or more accessible? Remember we’re looking for solutions that demonstrate impact, increase financial inclusion and drive innovation to build the future of finance

Remember you must bring laptops - no equipment supplied and all code must be written on the day.
