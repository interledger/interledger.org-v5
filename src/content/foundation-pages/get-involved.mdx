Interledger brings together developers, researchers, advocates, policymakers, educators, students and activists working on the same challenge: to build financial systems that are open, interoperable, and designed to work for everyone.

## Find Your Path

There are four ways to get involved, and most people move between them over time. Start wherever fits, and follow the threads that interest you.

We're also launching a new Membership program soon to bring our community together in new dynamic ways. If you want to be a founding member and help us build, please fill out this interest form, and we'll be in touch.

</Paragraph>

### **Just Getting Started?**

If you are new and not sure where you fit, the best first step is to [join the Interledger Slack](https://join.slack.com/t/interledger/shared_invite/zt-44g089zrn-XdSIiHF~cs8Oo_MBmSfECA) and introduce yourself. The community spans dozens of countries and a wide range of disciplines. Join us and say hi, the community team is happy to welcome and support your journey.

Alternatively , a great way to learn more about Interledger is by **joining one of our Community Calls**.  It’s the easiest way to find out more about us and the work being undertaken by our community.

They’re hosted on the second Wednesday of each month, at 3:00 PM UTC.

Please join us on Google Meet using code: **enb-ayiv-rnw**
