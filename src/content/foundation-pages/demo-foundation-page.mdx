<Paragraph>

This demo page gives editors and developers a compact preview of the content blocks available on foundation pages. The placeholder copy is intentionally specific enough to test typography, links, lists, inline emphasis, and longer sentences without implying that the details are final editorial content.

Blocks covered on this page:

- Paragraph (lists, links, footnotes)
- Split layout (image/video + text/quote)
- Profile grid
- Blockquote
- CTA strip
- PDF embed and video embed
- **Card grid** — all variants: Title, Resource, Info, Navigation
- Logo carousel

Use this paragraph to review standard prose spacing, inline formatting, and footnotes.[^foundation-demo]

## Event card

Foundation pages can use a full-width **Event card** with When and Where
columns, plus an optional Apply column. Editors should place one card at a
time — there is no multi-card grid. When Apply is omitted, When and Where
expand to fill the available width.

</Paragraph>

<Faq heading="About the Interledger Foundation">

<FaqItem question="What is the Interledger Foundation?">

The Interledger Foundation is a private operating foundation incorporated in California, United States. We steward open payment technologies, including the Interledger Protocol, the Open Payments API, and the Web Monetization technology standard.

We are not a financial institution, we do not operate a payment network, and we do not earn revenue from payments.

</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Who can apply for a grant?">

Applications are open to individuals, teams, and organisations worldwide. Each programme sets its own eligibility criteria, so check the [grant overview](/grant) for the programme you are interested in.

</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="Is the Interledger Foundation a cryptocurrency initiative?">

No. We are not a cryptocurrency, we do not have a token, and our technology does not use blockchain.

</FaqItem>

</Faq>
