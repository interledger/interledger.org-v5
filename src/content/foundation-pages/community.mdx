<Paragraph>

Interledger brings together developers, researchers, advocates, policymakers, educators, students and activists working on the same challenge: to build financial systems that are open, interoperable, and designed to work for everyone.

## Find Your Path

There are four ways to get involved, and most people move between them over time. Start wherever fits, and follow the threads that interest you.

We're also launching a new Membership program soon to bring our community together in new dynamic ways. If you want to be a founding member and help us build, please fill out this interest form, and we'll be in touch.

</Paragraph>

<CardGrid ariaLabel="Ways to get involved with the Interledger community" variant="Info" columns="Two">
<InfoCard heading="Build">

_For the people who make payments actually move._

If you write code, run a wallet, or just want to see how far you can push Open Payments in a weekend, this is your corner. Get one payment working, then find out what you can build on top of it.

- Explore [our technology](/tech/overview)
- [Developers' Portal](/tech/dev-portal)
- Get started with [Open Payments](https://openpayments.dev/overview/getting-started/)
- Browse the [repositories on GitHub](https://github.com/interledger)
- [Arrange an integration call](/contact)

</InfoCard>
<InfoCard heading="Connect">

_Technology alone doesn't create change, people do._

The community spans dozens of countries and every discipline that touches financial access, from code to policy to research to organizing on the ground. Come introduce yourself and see what everyone is working on.

- Join the [Community Slack](https://join.slack.com/t/interledger/shared_invite/zt-44g089zrn-XdSIiHF~cs8Oo_MBmSfECA)
- Join a [community call](/events)
- [Meet us at an event](/events) near you

</InfoCard>
<InfoCard heading="Learn">

_It all makes more sense once you see how the pieces fit._

Dig into how open payments work, why the system is built this way, who it impacts most, and where it is heading, straight from the people building it.

- Listen to the [Future|Money Podcast](/podcasts)
- Read [the blog](/blog)
- Check out our [Research and Report Library](/research-and-reports)
- [Subscribe to the newsletter](/subscribe)

</InfoCard>
<InfoCard heading="Support">

_Open systems do not build themselves_

Support our mission and our work - through a partnership, by applying for grant, or making a donation - and help it reach the people traditional finance leaves out.

- [Partner with us](/contact)
- [Explore grant funding](/grant/our-grantmaking/) for your work

</InfoCard>
</CardGrid>
