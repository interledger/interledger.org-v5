Hacktober 2026 has wrapped up, and it was a big success! Throughout October (October 1 to 31), builders and open-source enthusiasts from around the globe joined the Interledger Foundation to strengthen our open-source ecosystem. Thank you to everyone who took part.

## About Hacktober

[Hacktoberfest](https://hacktoberfest.com/) is an annual event that promotes open-source contributions throughout October. It aims to support and give back to open-source projects that underpin today’s tech infrastructure. Over the past decade, thousands of coders and non-coders have joined to support their favorite projects, improve their skills, and connect with like-minded open-source enthusiasts. We expressed our support for this initiative by running a Hacktober program of activities to engage the open source community.

</Paragraph>

<Paragraph>

## Thank you for your contributions!

Every pull request and bug report made our projects better. We are grateful for the time and care this community put into Hacktober 2026.

## Hacktober 2026 by the numbers

We closed the month with **260 total submissions**. Alongside that:

- 127 merged pull requests across 11 repositories
- 29 unique participants
- 116 Bug / Suggestion Form submissions
