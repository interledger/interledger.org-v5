Get ready for Hacktoberfest 2026! Throughout October (October 1–31), join the Interledger Foundation in strengthening our open-source ecosystem. We are excited to collaborate with builders, developers, and enthusiasts around the globe!

## About Hacktoberfest

[Hacktoberfest](https://hacktoberfest.com/) is an annual event that promotes open-source contributions throughout October. It aims to support and give back to open-source projects that underpin today’s tech infrastructure. Over the past decade, thousands of coders and non-coders have joined to support their favorite projects, improve their skills, and connect with like-minded open-source enthusiasts.

### **2. Bug Squashing on Our New Website**

We just launched our new website! Help us polish and refine the user experience! Submit your findings, feedback, and issue reports to our [Hacktoberfest Feedback Form](https://forms.gle/UCfks4TSFivDKa5L8).

What to look for:

- Broken or misdirected links
- Grammatical typos or formatting errors
- Usability suggestions and structural fixes

**Step-by-step Process:**

1. Step 1: Head to [https://interledger.org](https://interledger.org)
2. Step 2: Peruse, poke around, test things out!
3. Step 3: Report any bugs, suggestions or fixes using [this form](https://forms.gle/UCfks4TSFivDKa5L8)

**Contribution Guidelines and AI Policy**

We value original thought, creative thinking, and detailed specificity. Participants are welcome to use AI tools, however, submissions that consist mostly or entirely of unedited, generic AI-generated content will not be accepted. Please review our  [Interledger Foundation Programs AI Policies & Guidelines ](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1gpln1E3NKQSaxoD1yza02ZeniIuBs6rfETyYDWJYPm4/edit?usp=sharing).

**A qualifying pull request (PR) must:**

- Directly tackle and resolve one of the open issues listed in the event repositories.
- Demonstrate original thought, genuine effort, and personal understanding rather than unedited, generic AI-generated content ('AI slop').
- Adhere to the Interledger Foundation AI policies and guidelines.
