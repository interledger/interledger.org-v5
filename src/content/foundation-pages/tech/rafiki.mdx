<Paragraph>

## Built for Regulated Financial Institutions

Rafiki is designed for financial service providers operating under applicable financial regulation, not for individual developers or unregulated platforms. Interledger-powered networks built on Rafiki require participants to be licensed entities, with KYC and AML/CFT obligations, sanctions screening, and fraud prevention expected of every provider that connects. Those requirements are what let the network operate with confidence across regulated financial environments.

</Paragraph>
