<Paragraph>

### Your Creative Brief

Your mission, should you choose to accept it, is to design an end-of-year card and/or sticker that puts your own spin on Interledger Community. Celebrate the connections we've made, the things we've built together, or the possibilities ahead. 

Make it clever, funny, cute, heartfelt, wonderfully nerdy, or something no one has thought of yet.

We've packed a creative toolkit with templates, colors, illustrations and graphic elements to help you get inspired, although we encourage you to take the design in your own direction. You can choose **any medium** to create your designs: digital, painted, photography, hand-printed, the list goes on… Just make something you'd be proud to share, send, or stick on your laptop.

To ensure inclusive holiday messaging across global cultures, all submissions must align with an inclusive "end of year" theme (e.g., no Christmas-specific imagery) and should be aligned with our community mission of advancing open payment infrastructure and financial inclusion. 

- **Theme ideas:** Connectedness, open technology, new beginnings, global nodes
- Designs should focus on global reflection, gratitude, community, and looking forward to the new year.
- **Exclusions:** Avoid specific religious-only imagery to ensure the designs are inclusive across cultures, holidays, and secular celebrations worldwide.

</Paragraph>
