## Your Voice, From Day One

Open source development works because people take part. They ask questions, challenge assumptions, build things, and share what they learn. 

It’s the ethos behind the development of Interledger tech and the pursuit of our mission: to build the tech, grow the movement, and shift the system to unlock financial agency for all.

It’s also how we are going to create and run our Membership program - co-creation, with a direct, two-way line between members and our team
