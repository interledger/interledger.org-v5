## Your Voice, From Day One

Open source development works because people take part. They ask questions, challenge assumptions, build things, and share what they learn. 

It’s the ethos behind the development of Interledger tech and the pursuit of our mission: to build the tech, grow the movement, and shift the system to unlock financial agency for all. It’s also how we are going to create and run our Membership program: through co-creation, with a direct, two-way line between members and our team

Our first milestone is 500 members. Here’s what will happen when we get there:

## Who Membership is For

Membership is free, but we ask for something in return: **your participation**. It is a good fit if you:

- Believe money should move like data, and that opportunity should reach the people still left out of the financial system.
- Want to build, learn, research, design, teach, or advocate for open payments.
- Will take action when invited: reply to an email, share an idea, cast a vote, join a call.

</Paragraph>

<Faq intro={{"heading":"Membership FAQs"}}>

<FaqItem question="Does it cost anything?">

No. Membership is free.

</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="What do you expect from members?">

Mostly, that you take part: read our emails and updates, reply, share ideas, and vote when we ask.

</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="How is this different from the newsletter or Slack?">

Those are open to everyone and mostly one-way, with us sharing out updates about our work. Membership is smaller and two-way, with personal emails, opportunities matched to your interests, and a vote on what comes next.

</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="What happens after I join?">

You will get a welcome email, your digital badge, and updates from our team based on the interests you share with us.

</FaqItem>

<FaqItem question="How will you use my information?">

We ask you to provide some basic information when you sign up. We’ll use this to tailor how we reach you and to help us prioritize what to build for members.

</FaqItem>

</Faq>
