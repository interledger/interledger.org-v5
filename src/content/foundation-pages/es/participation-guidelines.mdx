The Interledger Foundation (ILF) welcomes everyone who shares our vision of an open, fair, and financially inclusive web to apply for our applicable [grant opportunities](https://interledger.org/opportunities) and/or [engage with members](https://community.interledger.org/) of our ecosystem. These Participation Guidelines are intended to create a safe and positive community experience for us all, regardless of:

- Ancestry
- Family status
- Medical condition
- Gender identity or expression
- Military or veteran status
- Sex
- Sexual orientation
- Native language
- Age
- Disability
- Race and/or ethnicity
- National origin
- Socioeconomic status
- Religion
- Geographic location

We welcome individuals into our space who will appreciate and accommodate our diverse cultural practices, attitudes, and beliefs, and maintain decorum that is

- Respectful and Inclusive 
- Enabling new perspectives to be heard
- Facilitating an openness to learn from others

## **Unacceptable Behavior**

The following will not be tolerated in the Interledger community, online or in person: violence and threats of violence; personal attacks; bullying; doxing; derogatory language; threats; hate speech; promoting harmful or illegal activities; unwelcome sexual attention or physical contact; non-consensual sharing of adult content; disruptive behavior, or influencing unacceptable behavior; or any other conduct that ILF deems as offensive or inappropriate to protect community members.

### **Consequences of Unacceptable Behavior**

Unacceptable behavior from anyone involved in the ILF community, including those with decision-making authority, will not be tolerated and will be dealt with appropriately.<br />‍<br />Violation of these guidelines can result in being banned from online and physical spaces and can lead to the cancellation of a grant, contract, award, stipend, or partnership.<br />‍<br />Program staff or contractors in violation of these guidelines may be subject to further consequences, such as disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment or contract.<br /><br />ILF reserves the right to address unacceptable behavior at any time at its discretion.
