### **Reflections from FOCUS: The Future of CDFIs Unleashed  Summit**

I recently attended the Future of CDFIs Unleashed Summit (FOCUS),  a 2.5 day convening sponsored by the Interledger Foundation in partnership with the Atlanta University Center Consortium (AUCC) Data Science Initatives (DSI).

Unlike many conferences, this gathering was intentionally small to foster honest conversation. Led by Casey Diké of Blaze Group and our wonderful Programs team at the Interledger Foundation, the event brought together Community Development Financial Institutions (CDFIs), fintech leaders, educators, and college students to explore an increasingly important question: _**how do we build financial systems that meet the realities of today's economy?**_
