Open technology is about more than protocols, APIs, and repositories. It's about the people who imagine them, build them, document them, and grow the communities that bring them to life.

That's why we launched **Off The Ledger!&#x20;**&#x49;t's the Interledger Foundation podcast exploring the technology, people, and ideas shaping the future of open payments.

Whether you're a developer curious about Open Payments, a technical writer passionate about documentation, a community builder, or simply interested in the future of the web, Off The Ledger offers an inside look at the work happening across the Interledger ecosystem.

Each episode features engineers, product leaders, technical writers, community advocates, and contributors who share not only _what_ they're building, but _why_ it matters.

</Paragraph>

## Looking Ahead

As the Interledger ecosystem continues to grow, so will the conversations.

While Season 1 introduced many of the technologies that power Interledger, future episodes will expand beyond products to explore the people and ideas behind them. We'll dive into topics like engineering leadership, developer experience, technical writing, AI, open standards, financial inclusion, community building, and the challenges of creating technology that's open by design.

Because building the future of payments isn't just about writing code, it's about sharing knowledge, learning from one another, and asking better questions.

## Help Shape Future Episodes

One of the best parts of open source is that everyone has a voice and we'd love yours to be part of the conversation.

Is there a technology you'd like us to explain? A contributor you'd love to hear from? A topic you'd like us to unpack? Or perhaps a question you've always wanted to ask the engineers building the future of open payments? Share your ideas with us.

Whether you're interested in protocol design, engineering culture, interoperability, financial inclusion, AI, developer tooling, or anything in between, we're always looking for topics that matter to our community.

After all, **Off The Ledger** is more than a podcast! It's a conversation. And the best conversations are the ones we build together.
