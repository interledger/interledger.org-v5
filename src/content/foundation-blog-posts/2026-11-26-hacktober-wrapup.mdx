## **The Vision: Opening the Front Door to Our Ecosystem**

At the Interledger Foundation, our core mission is making global financial systems open and fair for everyone. We steward open payment technology, support builders expanding financial access, and work to transform how money moves across the web. But open-source technology is only as strong as the community behind it.

As October approached, we set out with a clear goal: build welcoming, accessible pathways for developers, designers, and enthusiasts worldwide to make meaningful contributions to the Interledger ecosystem. Whether fixing critical bugs, refining documentation, or testing our newly launched website, we wanted every participant to find a place where their effort truly mattered.

## **The Plot Twist: Overcoming Early Hurdles**

No open source campaign comes without plot twists, and Hacktober 2026 was no exception!

Right at the starting line, we hit an unexpected speed bump — a sudden branding pivot that required us to shift our campaign name overnight to **Hacktober**. While adjusting branding on the fly was a challenge, our team rapidly recalibrated, updated our messaging, and launched on schedule.

Then came our second challenge — one we were thrilled to have! Within days of starting the campaign, community interest exploded. 

What started as a small list of issues quickly ran out as the wave of incoming pull requests intensified. To meet the demand, we expanded the scope to include virtually any existing or newly created issue across our repositories — regardless of outcome, whether it led to a full resolution or served as an exploration or analysis. 

What amazed us even more was seeing participants proactively find their own bugs and submit direct fixes for them. To keep up with the momentum, our engineering and program teams spent extra time scavenging repositories and creating fresh task pathways on the fly so no eager contributor was left empty-handed.

## **The Result: Unstoppable Community Momentum**

What began as a month-long experiment in open community engagement turned into one of our most successful initiatives yet. The enthusiasm across our repositories and feedback channels exceeded all expectations.

## **Key Campaign Impact**

- **260 Qualifying Submissions:** Participants contributed across both GitHub repositories and detailed website feedback submissions.
- **127 Merged Pull Requests:** Spanning 11 core repositories, including Publisher Tools, Web Monetization, TestNet, Rhyza, and Interledger Pay.
- **116 Bug Reports & Usability Enhancements:** Submitted via our Google Form to help polish our brand-new website.
- **29 Active Code Contributors:** Bringing diverse skill levels and fresh perspectives into our developer community.
- **66.67% Participant Return Rate:** 20 contributors returned to complete two or more contributions throughout the month, showing real sustained engagement.
