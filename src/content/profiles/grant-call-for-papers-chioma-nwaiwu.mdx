Chioma Nwaiwu builds on earlier national survey data to study forcibly displaced persons in Nigeria. She maps gaps in financial inclusion across seven conflict-affected states and examines how open payments could help close them, including whether displaced people can continue to use their accounts and receive money after they move.

- Paper: [Financial Inclusion at the Margins: Forcibly Displaced Persons, Gender Gaps and the Potential of Open Payment Interoperability in Nigeria](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/417571647_Financial_Inclusion_at_the_Margins_Forcibly_Displaced_Persons_Gender_Gaps_and_the_Potential_of_Open_Payment_Interoperability_in_Nigeria)
- Blog post: **For Nigeria's Displaced People, the Problem Isn't Access. It's Whether Money Follows Them:**[https://interledger.org/blog/whether-money-follows-them/](/blog/whether-money-follows-them/)
