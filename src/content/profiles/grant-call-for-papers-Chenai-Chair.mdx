Chenai Chair and Chiedza Kehle study the lived realities of immigrants in Gauteng, South Africa, who send money home through remittance platforms. Using a feminist, intersectional lens, they assess three platforms, Mukuru, Mama Money and HelloPaisa, on the value they offer users and on their data governance. <br /> 

Their paper, Data for Financial Inclusion?, was published on My Data Rights Africa in August 2026, together with an educational game based on the traditional children's game Nhodo.<br />Final paper published on My data Rights Africa: [https://mydatarights.africa/data-for-financial-inclusion/](https://mydatarights.africa/data-for-financial-inclusion/)
