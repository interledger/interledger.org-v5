### Digital Public Infrastructure's Promise of Financial Inclusion and the Reality of Digital Lending: A Comparative Approach

Mila T. Samdub and Dr. Tanushree Kaushal examine the promises and limits of India's digital public infrastructure (DPI) for financial inclusion. They compare DPI-enabled digital lending with microfinance and public banking, focusing on trust, social ties and institutional credibility, and ask why digital lending reaches comparatively few women.
